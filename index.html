Kyle Lord.

The Pocket Crew is built by Kyle Lord — working filmmaker and photographer based in Melbourne, Australia. Seventeen years shooting commercial, documentary, editorial, event and social content across every kind of brief.

The work's taken me from tight one-person shoots with a single camera and a Peak Design bag, to larger crews on the road for multi-day commercial productions. Solo operator on most jobs, part of a small crew on the rest.

Everything in The Pocket Crew comes from that actual workflow — tools I'd built for myself on real jobs, cleaned up and shipped so other solo shooters don't have to build them from scratch.
